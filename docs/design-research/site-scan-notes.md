## Site-by-Site Analysis

### 1. aboutluca.com — Luca Nardi, Creative Developer & Designer
**Status:** ✅ Done

**Visual Signature:**
- Pure black background
- 3D dot/particle field occupying bottom 40% of viewport — particles form shapes/text
- Cinematic loading screen: minimal "LOADING" text centered with thin horizontal rule
- ENTER gate ritual — same minimal treatment
- "SCROLL TO EXPLORE" text FORMED BY the particle field itself (particles rearrange into letterforms)

**Navigation:**
- Fixed minimal: name top-left, rounded pill CTA top-right ("LET'S TALK")
- Social links bottom-left (small caps, muted)
- Sound toggle available

**Interactions:**
- Scroll drives 3D camera through WebGL particle field
- Cursor moves particles
- 24 project items in DOM, revealed via scroll

**Typography:**
- Spaced-out all-caps for labels
- Serif for name/logo
- Monochromatic — no color accent needed

**Key Insight for CoE-EA:**
- Particles forming text: our field could form domain names or stats as you hover/dwell
- Loading ritual creates anticipation — not a template site signal
- Letting the field BE the hero — no hero image needed
- Pill CTA style works well against dark particle backgrounds

### 2. meteo.ashwyn.studio — ashMeteo

**Visual Signature:**
